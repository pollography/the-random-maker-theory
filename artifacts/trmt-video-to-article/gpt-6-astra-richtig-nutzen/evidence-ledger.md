## Contradictions

OpenAI's current official guidance is narrower: Low or Medium is a starting point, higher effort can consume more allowance, and no level guarantees a better result. The public article follows the official boundary.
- The private source leans toward removing broad skill systems. OpenAI recommends auditing skills and instructions because Astra is sensitive to them. The public article keeps, tests, updates, or disables skills instead of deleting indiscriminately.
